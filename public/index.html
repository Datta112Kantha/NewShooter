How to Load the Extension in Google Chrome

  1. 1
    Download and extract the ZIP archive

    Click the Download .ZIP Extension button above. Once downloaded, extract bin4ry-relay-extension.zip to a permanent folder on your computer.

  2. 2
    Open the Chrome Extensions management page

    Open a new tab in Google Chrome, navigate to chrome://extensions in your address bar, and press Enter.

    chrome://extensions
  3. 3
    Turn ON "Developer mode"

    Locate the Developer mode toggle switch in the top-right corner of the Extensions page and toggle it ON.

  4. 4
    Click "Load unpacked" and select the unzipped folder

    Click the Load unpacked button in the top-left toolbar. In the file picker, select the extracted extension folder (the folder containing manifest.json).

  5. 5
    Pin and verify the extension

    Click the Extensions icon (🧩) in the Chrome toolbar and pin Bin4ry Relay Controller. Open its popup to verify the WebSocket hub is connected.
